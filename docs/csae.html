Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

PeerFocus is committed to maintaining a safe environment and has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Last updated: January 2026

1. Prohibited Content and Behaviour

PeerFocus strictly prohibits any content or activity that involves child sexual abuse or exploitation. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Any sexual content involving minors (anyone under 18), including images, videos, audio, text, or links.
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content that sexualises minors.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or attempts to obtain sexual content from a minor.
  • Sexual extortion, trafficking, or coercion involving minors.
  • Sharing, requesting, or directing users to obtain CSAM or exploitative content.
  • Any content that encourages, depicts, or facilitates CSAE.

If you believe a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services immediately.

2. Detection and Moderation

PeerFocus uses a combination of measures to help detect and address prohibited content, including:

  • User reporting tools inside the app.
  • Account and content review workflows for reports.
  • Enforcement actions against accounts that violate these standards.

Note: No system is perfect, but we continuously improve safety measures and respond promptly to reports.

3. Reporting CSAE Content

If you see content or behaviour that may involve CSAE, you can report it in any of the following ways:

  • In-app reporting: Use the Report option available on messages, user profiles, or community content (where available).
  • Email: Contact us at support@peer-focus.com with the subject line “CSAE Report”.

What to include in a report

  • The username(s) involved (and any community name if relevant).
  • What happened and when it occurred.
  • Screenshots and/or relevant message details (if safe to provide).

Please do not share illegal content in your email. Describe it and provide context instead.

4. Enforcement

Violations of these CSAE standards may result in one or more of the following actions:

  • Immediate removal of content.
  • Suspension or permanent termination of the offending account(s).
  • Blocking device access where appropriate.
  • Preservation of relevant records for legal and safety purposes.
  • Reporting to appropriate authorities, including where required by law.

5. Cooperation With Law Enforcement and Legal Compliance

PeerFocus may report apparent CSAE/CSAM to relevant authorities where required or appropriate, and may cooperate with lawful requests consistent with applicable law.

6. Child Safety and Age Requirements

PeerFocus is intended for users aged 13 and older (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not allow content that exploits or endangers minors.
